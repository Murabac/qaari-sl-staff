FULL DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------
Xulka Quraada Staff is the internal companion app for the Xulka Quraada archive.

Production team members can create reciters, upload surah audio, and submit work for review. Admins can approve or reject recitations (including voice notes) and manage ayah timing sync so listeners get accurate follow-along text.

WHO IT’S FOR
• Production — upload and submit recitations
• Admin / Super Admin — review queue, reject with notes, ayah sync

This app is for authorized staff only. Listener browsing and playback use the public Xulka Quraada app.

Privacy: https://qaari.mahaysaa.com/privacy
